## ABOUT ME
I'm a Data Analyst focused on turning data into clear, actionable insights that support better business decisions.

I work with SQL, Power BI, and Excel to analyze and transform data, uncover trends and patterns, build interactive dashboards, and communicate findings clearly to stakeholders.

I believe effective data analysis goes beyond reporting numbers. It starts with asking the right questions, understanding the business context, and identifying what the data is really telling us. I aim to turn complex data into practical insights that help teams understand performance, identify opportunities, and make informed decisions.
